ModHub: Happy Mod Pro Privacy Policy

We do not collect, store, use, or share any personal data or sensitive user data.

The App is designed to function entirely on your device without transmitting any of your personal information to our servers or any third-party servers. You can use the App without providing any personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

Device Permissions

The ModHub: Happy Mod Pro App may request certain device permissions (such as access to local storage) strictly to provide the core functionality of the features within the software.

  • Any data processed through these permissions remains locally on your device.
  • We do not extract, monitor, or upload this data.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services, trackers, analytics tools, or advertising networks that collect your personal data.

Children’s Privacy

Because we do not collect any personal information, our App inherently complies with global children's privacy regulations, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or any other age).

Changes to This Privacy Policy
